How often are exchange rates updated?

Rates come from Frankfurter's exchange-rate data and are updated according to its underlying official providers. They are suitable for general conversion, not live trading.

Is Currenzy free?

Yes. The converter and currency directory are free to use.

What does an ISO currency code mean?

An ISO 4217 code is a three-letter identifier for a currency, such as USD for the US Dollar and EGP for the Egyptian Pound.

Are these rates for trading or payments?

No. They are reference/mid-market rates for general information. Banks, card networks and money-transfer services can apply their own rates and fees.
